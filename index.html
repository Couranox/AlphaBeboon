Camera Controls

  • W, A, S, D / Arrow Keys: Pan camera
  • Q / E: Rotate camera view
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in / out
  • Spacebar (Tap): Toggle top-down vs angled view
  • Spacebar (Hold) + Mouse Wheel: Adjust camera pitch angle
  • ` (Backtick): Select King. Double-tap to focus camera.
  • M, B, V, K: Select Market, Barracks, Siege Shop, Keep. Double-tap to focus.

Unit Controls

  • Left Click: Select unit / building / confirm placement
  • Double Left Click: Select all of same kind on screen
  • Left Click & Drag: Box select Military
  • Ctrl + Drag: Box select Peasants
  • Alt + Drag: Box select Buildings
  • Ctrl + Alt + Drag: Box select all Mobile Units
  • Right Click: Move / Attack / Gather / Construct
  • Right Click & Drag: Custom formation movement
  • X: Stop selected units
  • F: Toggle Fight-Move (Attack-Move) mode
  • G: Toggle Attack Ground mode
  • Esc: Cancel placement / clear selection

Control Groups

  • Ctrl (or Alt) + [0-9]: Save selected units to group

  • [0-9]: Select group (tap twice to focus camera)

How to Play

The Basics

Welcome to Kingdom Strategy Game! Your goal is to build a thriving economy, train a powerful military, and defeat the enemy while protecting your own Keep. You start with a Keep, some initial resources, and a few peasants to get your kingdom rolling.

Economy & Gathering

Your peasants are the backbone of your empire. Left idle, they will wander around. Assign them to buildings by selecting a building and the peasants will automatically start working.

  • Food: Gathered from Farms. Used to feed your army and spawn new peasants at the Keep.
  • Premium Food: Bakes in the Bakery from normal Food. Feeding this to your people grants extra Gold per unit consumed.
  • Wood: Harvested by Woodcutters near trees. Crucial for building basic structures and ranged weapons.
  • Iron & Stone: Mined by placing Mines over mineral deposits. Iron is needed for advanced weapons and armor.
  • Gold: Earned from selling items at the Market or consuming Premium Food. Used to train soldiers and build advanced structures.

Military & Combat

To build an army, first construct a Barracks and various weaponsmiths (Fletcher for bows, Blacksmith for swords, Armorer for armor). Idle peasants will automatically deliver crafted weapons and armor to your Barracks.

Once equipped with gear in the Barracks, you can spend Gold to train peasants into specific soldier types. Don't forget to build Stables to breed horses for cavalry units!

Defenses: Protect your village by dragging walls. Walls can be elevated, and you can place Gatehouses to allow your units to pass through.

Logistics & Trade

Loadhouses: Used to transport large quantities of goods automatically using horse-drawn wagons. Perfect for moving resources from distant mines back to your Keep.

Markets: Allow you to quickly buy and sell resources using Gold to balance your economy. Watch the prices to make a profit!

Building Directory

A comprehensive guide to all structures you can build.

Keep: (Free.) Your central hub. Produces 1 Peasant every 8 seconds, up to 10 residents. Costs 10 food to spawn a Peasant.

House: (10 Wood.) Produces 1 Peasant every 8 seconds, up to 10 residents per house. Costs 10 food to spawn a Peasant.

Farm: (10 Wood, 1 Peasant.) Grows and harvests Food automatically over time.

Woodcutter: (5 Wood, 1 Peasant.) Harvests Wood from nearby trees.

Mine: (10 Wood, up to 3 Peasants.) Must be placed on ore deposits to gather Iron or Stone.

Barracks: (10 Stone.) Stores crafted weapons/armor and trains idle peasants into soldiers using Gold.

Poleturner: (10 Wood, 100 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Spears and Pikes. Spears (5 wood / 5s) or Pikes (10 wood / 10s).

Gruntshop: (20 Wood, 100 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Axes and Halberds. Axes (5 iron / 5s) or Halberds (10 wood, 5 iron / 15s).

Blacksmith: (20 Wood, 200 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Swords and Maces. Swords (10 iron / 10s) or Maces (10 iron / 10s).

Fletcher: (20 Wood, 100 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Shortbows and Longbows. Shortbows (10 wood / 10s) or Longbows (15 wood / 15s).

Knight Arms: (20 Wood, 200 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Poleaxes and Crossbows. Poleaxes (5 wood, 15 iron / 20s) or Crossbows (10 wood, 10 iron / 20s).

Tailor: (10 Wood, 100 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Cloth Armor and Leather Armor. Cloth Armor (5 food / 5s) or Leather Armor (10 food / 10s).

Armorer: (20 Wood, 200 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Produces Chainmail and Plate Armor. Chainmail (5 iron / 5s) or Plate Armor (10 iron / 10s).

Stables: (20 Wood, 10 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Breeds Horses (20 food / 20s) for your cavalry units.

Market: (20 Wood, 10 Gold.) Allows instantaneous buying and selling of resources for Gold.

Siege Shop: (15 Wood, 5 Iron, 100 Gold.) Trains idle peasants directly into Siege Weapons.

Loadhouse: (20 Wood.) Dispatches horse-drawn wagons to automatically collect resources from distant camps.

Baker: (20 Wood, 1 Peasant.) Converts 10 Food into 20 Premium Food (20s). Peasants will eat Premium Food instead of normal Food if they can, and will pay 1 gold per Premium Food consumed.

Brewery: (20 Wood, 100 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Converts 10 Food into Brew (20s). Brews sell for 40, buy for 80, and give 41 gold when consumed by population.

Carpenter: (20 Wood, 1 Peasant.) Converts 20 Wood into Furniture (20s). Furniture sells for 60, buys for 120, and gives 61 gold when consumed by population.

Jeweler: (20 Wood, 200 Gold, 1 Peasant.) Converts 20 Stone into 1 Gem (20s). Gems sell for 120, buy for 240, and give 121 gold when consumed by population.

Wall: (1 Stone.) Drag to place defensive walls. Units can stand on top for range and defense bonuses.

Gatehouse: (2 Stone.) Place Gatehouses to allow units through.
